## 1. Executive Summary

## 2. Resource Productivity Benchmarks

### 2.1 Water Productivity ($WP$)
Water Productivity measures crop yield produced per unit of total water applied (irrigation + effective rainfall):
$$WP = \frac{Y}{\text{Total Water Applied } (m^3)}$$

- **Conventional Fixed Schedule Baseline**: $0.95\text{ kg grain / } m^3$
- **AgriSense AI Precision Telemetry-Guided Field**: **$1.42\text{ kg grain / } m^3$**
- **Relative Productivity Gain**: **$+49.5\%$ improvement**

### 2.2 Fertilizer Use Efficiency ($FUE$)
Fertilizer Use Efficiency measures total grain yield generated per kilogram of synthetic NPK nutrients applied:
$$FUE = \frac{Y}{\text{Total NPK Nutrient Input } (\text{kg})}$$

- **Conventional Standard Application**: $18.5\text{ kg yield / kg NPK}$
- **AgriSense AI ICAR-Guided Dosing**: **$26.4\text{ kg yield / kg NPK}$**
- **Relative Efficiency Gain**: **$+42.7\%$ improvement**
